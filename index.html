How to choose the right Ozylix supplement

Ozylix builds supplements around the effervescent format because water-soluble delivery means higher absorption and easier daily use than hard-to-swallow tablets. Glutathione and vitamin C effervescent tablets suit skin and immunity goals; spirulina tablets with B12, D3, magnesium and iron cover daily greens; ashwagandha and ayurvedic blends like moringa support stress, energy and recovery; and multivitamin and energy formulas like Multidiata and Power Pro serve complete nutrition and stamina needs. As dietary supplements, our products support a balanced lifestyle — they are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.

Made in India, FSSAI approved

Every Ozylix batch is manufactured and lab tested at our own FSSAI-approved facility in Anand, Gujarat, and ships straight from the factory — no middlemen. Ozylix VitaPoints earn you 1 point per ₹1 spent, redeemable at 150 points = ₹1 off, and every order qualifies for free pan-India delivery.

Frequently asked questions

Are effervescent tablets better absorbed?

Dissolving into water releases nutrients in a readily soluble form, which is gentler on the stomach and easier for the body to use than compressed tablets. As always, results vary from person to person.

Accessibility Statement

Ozylix — Committed to inclusive digital access

Ozylix is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to people of all abilities, including those with disabilities. We strive to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards.

🎨
Visual Accessibility

High contrast colour ratios throughout the site. Text sizes are scalable and all images include descriptive alt text. We avoid relying solely on colour to convey information.

⌨️
Keyboard Navigation

All interactive elements are accessible via keyboard. Tab order follows a logical sequence and focus indicators are visible throughout the site.

📱
Mobile & Touch Accessible

Our site is fully responsive and designed for touch interfaces. Tap targets are adequately sized to ensure ease of use on mobile devices.

🔊
Screen Reader Support

Semantic HTML and ARIA labels are used throughout to support screen readers such as NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver on mobile devices.

🌐
Language & Readability

The site language is declared as English. Content is written in plain language. Line spacing and paragraph widths are optimised for readability.

🎬
Motion & Animation

Animations are kept subtle and non-essential. We respect the user's operating system preference for reduced motion via the prefers-reduced-motion CSS media query.

Known Limitations

We are continuously working to improve accessibility across all pages. Some third-party payment gateway elements (GoKwik) may not be fully under our control. We are working with our payment provider to ensure maximum accessibility compliance.
